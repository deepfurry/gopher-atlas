## Positioning

GopherAtlas is a curated atlas of quality writing for Go developers, focused on ideas with lasting value.

We prioritize careful selection, useful organization, and clear reading paths over the number of links collected. Curated articles lead to the original source; our text explains why a piece deserves your attention. Learning Notes are original writing, organized into groups for continuous reading.

## Selection standards

- Accurate technical explanations and reliable examples.
- Clear reasoning about principles, context, and trade-offs, beyond lists of APIs.
- Useful engineering insights, rather than marketing or shallow reposts.
- Lasting reference value.
- Preference for original authors, core contributors, and experienced practitioners.

Outdated, inaccurate, redundant, or low-value articles are not normally included.

## Ratings

Ratings indicate reading priority, not an absolute score or a guarantee that every claim is correct.

- `S+`: Exceptional lasting value; read first.
- `S`: Essential, high-quality writing; read in full.
- `A+`: Strong writing that extends a topic.
- `A`: Reliable supplementary reading.
- `B+ / B`: Useful references with a lower priority.
- `C+ / C`: Background or supplementary material.

We consider accuracy, depth, information density, readability, practical relevance, and lasting value.

## Three reading paths

Curated Articles link to external writing and provide editorial notes. Topics arrange curated articles into a recommended sequence. Learning Notes share original experience, organized into reading groups.

## Attribution and feedback

GopherAtlas helps you find original sources; it does not replace them. Third-party articles remain the work and property of their original authors and publishers.

Recommend an article or report a broken link or inaccurate description through the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/deepfurry/gopher-atlas).
